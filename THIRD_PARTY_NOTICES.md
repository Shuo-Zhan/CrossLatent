# Provenance and licensing

CrossLatent is a renamed core distribution of the authors' GraBSpArk 0.1.1 implementation. The original research workspace is preserved separately. The numerical selection and SAE definitions retain their original implementation; renaming does not create a new algorithm.

InterPLM, ERNIE-RNA and Evo 2 are external dependencies supplied by users. Their upstream licenses and checkpoint terms continue to apply. No upstream repository or pretrained checkpoint is bundled here. The local SAE definitions and adapters support the original checkpoint formats; authors should confirm the provenance and redistribution terms of any adapted implementation before public release.

The authors have not yet selected a license for this distribution or supplied complete citation metadata. This file is a provenance notice, not a license grant. Add an author-approved LICENSE and citation information before describing this repository as open source.
